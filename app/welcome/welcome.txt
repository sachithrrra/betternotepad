Welcome to Better Notepad

A plain-text editor that stays out of your way. No accounts, no cloud,
no formatting — just text, saved where you put it.

A few things worth knowing:

  - Tabs: Cmd+T for a new tab, Ctrl+Tab to switch, Cmd+W to close.
  - Find & replace: Cmd+F and Cmd+H. F3 jumps to the next match.
  - Go to line: Cmd+G.
  - Insert time/date: F5.
  - Zoom: Cmd+= / Cmd+- / Cmd+0.
  - Word wrap, status bar, font and theme live in Settings (Cmd+,).

Better Notepad opens any text file, keeps its encoding and line endings,
and shows up in Finder's "Open With" menu.

This file is yours — edit it, keep it as a scratchpad, or close it.
It won't come back.
